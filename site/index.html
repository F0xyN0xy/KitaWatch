Anime, locally.

A desktop anime streaming client for Windows and Linux. No central servers, no accounts, no ads — every backend runs on your machine. Streams resolve from a chain of independent providers with automatic failover, and the app keeps itself updated.

Download for Windows Linux builds

Free · Open source (non-commercial) · Auto-updating · ~15 s first-launch setup

Everything runs on your machine

KitaWatch ships with its own backends bundled — nothing to install, nothing to configure, nothing phoning home.

Seven-backend resolver

Kuhi, Anivexa, 1anime, AniKage, Consumet, animepahe and an optional torrent fallback — if one source fails, the next one catches.

A real player

HLS with server switching, fatal-error auto-failover, skip intro/outro (AniSkip), keyboard shortcuts, styled ASS subtitles, PiP and screenshots.

AniList, your way

Optional login to sync favorites both ways — you always authenticate with your own account. Works fully offline of it, too.

Local-first & private

All backends run on localhost. No analytics, no telemetry, no accounts. Your list and history never leave your device.

Self-updating

Cryptographically signed updates from GitHub Releases. Install once — stay current forever.

See what works

A built-in provider probe shows which backends carry any anime before you press play.

No servers of ours

KitaWatch is a client application. It does not host, store, or distribute content; playback sources are resolved from third parties at runtime on your device.
