KitaWatch is a desktop anime client built to keep the experience close to home: your library and history stay on your device, with optional AniList sync and a player that can switch sources when one fails.

Made for desktopLocal-first by design

Less platform. More personal library.

The useful parts of a watch client, without needing an account just to get started.

A

One player, multiple sources

Switch sources and let playback try another provider when a stream fails.

B

Keep track your way

Manage watch status locally and optionally sync your list with AniList.

C

Subtitles you can tune

Styled subtitles, playback shortcuts, skip-intro support, and picture-in-picture.

D

Local-first by default

The app's backend services run on your own machine. KitaWatch does not operate a central streaming server.

E

Updates without the hunt

Install a supported release and use the built-in updater to stay current.

F

Know what is responding

Provider status and troubleshooting tools help narrow down playback problems.

Using a phone or tablet?

A native mobile version is on the roadmap, but it is not available to download yet. KitaWatch currently ships desktop builds only.

Follow mobile progress

No central streaming server

KitaWatch is a client. Playback sources are provided by third parties and resolved at runtime on your device.
